## Why do we use these components?

Among the most notable components of our project, we highlight the use of:
- **Nvidia Jetson AGX Orin**: This is the device that runs the AI models locally and performs most of the heavy-duty calculations; we utilize acceleration and optimization through the ***CUDA*** platform to take advantage of the GPU.
- **HMI Display**: As Human-Machine Interface technology, it enables interactive experiences since it acts as both an input and output device at the same time.
- **ESP32**: This is the microcontroller we use in each of our units because it allows us to utilize the **IoT** platform natively; furthermore, its 240 MHz clock speed enables us to perform heavy computations.
